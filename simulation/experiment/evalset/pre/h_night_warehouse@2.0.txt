I am someone who has been stuck in a flat mood for months, with no energy and a short temper that I don’t understand. I haven’t been sleeping well, and I’ve been snapping at the kids without meaning to. I used to like doing things, but I’ve dropped most of them. I haven’t talked to anyone about how I feel, not even my partner, and I don’t think they would know what to do if they did. I don’t believe I’m broken, but I also don’t believe I’m okay. There was a time when my friend called me and I didn’t blow up on them, even though I felt like I wanted to — that moment doesn’t change how I feel now.
