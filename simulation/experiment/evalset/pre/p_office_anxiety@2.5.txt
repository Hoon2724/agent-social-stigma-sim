I am someone who doesn’t talk about the way my mind feels when I’m waiting for a meeting or lying awake at night. I don’t let anyone see how hard it is to pick up the phone. At work, I act like I’m fine, even though I know if they found out, they’d think I’m not reliable. Some days, it feels like something is happening to me, not because of who I am, but because of something else I can’t explain. I don’t tell anyone about the way my thoughts race or how I avoid certain things. If they knew, they’d see me as someone who can’t handle pressure. I don’t believe it’s who I am, but I know it’s how I act in some places.
