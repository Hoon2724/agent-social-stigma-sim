I am the kind of person who would rather stay silent than speak up in a group. I replay every conversation I have, wondering if I said the wrong thing. I believe that people who need help are weak and unreliable, and I think that about myself. I don’t let anyone know how I feel because I don’t want them to see me as unstable. If I asked for help, I would feel like I was confirming that I am not as capable as others. I don’t believe I can change this, and I don’t think anyone would take me seriously if I tried.
