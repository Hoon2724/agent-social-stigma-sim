I feel panicky during rushes, my hands shake and I have to step out back to calm down. I dread the dinner shift and it’s been going on for about a year. I haven’t talked to anyone about it or seen a doctor. I don’t know if it’s something that can be fixed or if I just have to keep pushing through. I know that once, when I was really shaking during a rush, my manager didn’t say anything and just let me take a break without asking questions. I don’t know if that means I’m not as broken as I feel or if I just got lucky. I don’t think anyone would treat me any differently if they knew, but I also don’t think they would really understand what it’s like.
