There were times I stayed away from things because I didn’t want to be the kind of person who needed help — like maybe there was something wrong with me that I couldn’t just fix on my own. I kept quiet for a long while, pretending I was fine, even when I wasn’t. I was scared that if I talked to someone, they’d think I was weak or broken or not worth fixing. Eventually, I went to see a counsellor, just like I’d told myself I would never do. The appointment was quiet, mostly me talking, mostly me not knowing what to say. What I found out wasn’t what I feared — it wasn’t about being broken. It was about being human. I still don’t have all the answers, but I know now that needing help doesn’t mean I’m not enough.
