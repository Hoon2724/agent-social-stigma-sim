There were times I’d sit at my desk for hours, staring at a blank page, my hands shaking so badly I couldn’t even type. I’d tell myself it was just stress, that I’d handle it on my own — asking for help felt like admitting I wasn’t enough. I was scared that if I went to someone, they’d see something broken in me, something that meant I wasn’t who I thought I was. Eventually, I went to a counsellor. The first time I sat down with them, I just kept my eyes on the floor, waiting for the judgment I was sure was coming. What they saw wasn’t what I feared — they saw someone trying to hold it all together, and they stayed with me until I didn’t have to anymore. Now, I still feel the weight of it sometimes, but I know I don’t have to carry it alone.
