There were times I felt like I had to handle it all on my own — like if I asked for help, it would mean I wasn’t strong enough, or that I was broken in some way I couldn’t fix. I worried that seeing someone professional would confirm that there was something wrong with me, something I couldn’t explain but that would make me feel even more out of place. Eventually, I went to a counsellor, just once, not sure what I was looking for. What I found wasn’t what I expected — it wasn’t a label or a diagnosis, but a quiet understanding that I wasn’t alone in feeling this way. Now, I still feel it sometimes, but I don’t let it define me the way I used to.
