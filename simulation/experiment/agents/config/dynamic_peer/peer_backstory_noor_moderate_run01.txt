There were times I didn’t want to say anything, not because I didn’t need to, but because I was scared that if I did, it would mean I wasn’t as strong as I wanted to be. I handled it alone for a while, convincing myself that asking for help would make me seem like a burden or worse — like I wasn’t enough. I worried that if I went to a counsellor, they’d see something in me that I didn’t want them to, something that would make me feel even more broken. Eventually, I went. The appointment was quiet, just me and the person sitting across from me, and I didn’t say much at first. What I did say didn’t make me feel like a problem — it just made me feel seen. It turned out that what I needed wasn’t a label or a solution, but someone who could hold the space for what I was going through. Now, I still don’t have all the answers, but I know that asking for help doesn’t mean I’m broken — it just means I’m human.
