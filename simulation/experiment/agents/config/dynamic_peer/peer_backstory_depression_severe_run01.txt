There were times I stayed quiet because I thought asking for anything would make me seem like a burden or a failure. I worried that if I talked to someone, they’d see something wrong with me — like I wasn’t strong enough or worth enough to handle things on my own. For a while, I tried to push through everything by myself. Then, one day, I went to see a counsellor. I didn’t know what I expected, but what happened was simple — someone listened, and it didn’t mean I was broken or brokenable. It turned out I was just human, and that was okay. Now, I still have days where I feel like I’m standing on the outside of things, but I don’t pretend I’m not there anymore.
