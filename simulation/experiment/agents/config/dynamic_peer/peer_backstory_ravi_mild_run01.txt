There were times I didn’t think I could keep going without breaking, but I stayed quiet because I was afraid that if I said anything, it would mean I wasn’t strong enough. I worried that if I went to someone for help, they’d see something wrong with me, like I was broken or not worth fixing. Eventually, I went to a counsellor, just like I’d always told myself I wouldn’t. The first time I sat there, I didn’t know what to say, but I stayed. What I found out was that I wasn’t broken — I was just carrying something heavy, and it was okay to ask for help. I still don’t have all the answers, but I’ve stopped pretending I have to carry it alone.
