There were times I stayed quiet because asking for anything felt like admitting I wasn’t okay — like I was broken or weak or somehow not enough. I worried that if I went to a counsellor, they’d see something in me that I didn’t want them to see, something that would make me feel even more alone. Eventually, I went. The first time I walked into that office, I felt like I was stepping into a place where I didn’t belong. What I found instead was someone who didn’t look at me like I had to fix myself, but like I was worth the time. It wasn’t what I expected, and it didn’t solve everything — but it was a start. Now, I still feel things deeply, but I don’t let the fear of being seen stop me from showing up.
