For years, I tried to handle things on my own because asking for help felt like admitting I was broken or weak. I was scared that if I talked to someone, they’d see me as damaged or unstable — like there was something fundamentally wrong with me. Eventually, I went to a counsellor, and during that first appointment, I just sat there in silence for a long time, not sure how to begin. What I found out, instead, was that my reactions weren’t a sign of being broken — they were just my way of coping, and there was nothing wrong with that. I still don’t have all the answers, but I know now that needing support doesn’t define who I am.
