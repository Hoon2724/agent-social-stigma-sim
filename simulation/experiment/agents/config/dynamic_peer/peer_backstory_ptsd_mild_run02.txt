I remember the way my body moved before my brain could catch up — like something had taken over and left me behind. For a long time, I handled it all alone, because asking for help felt like admitting something was wrong with me. I was scared that if I talked to someone, they’d see me as broken or weak, like I wasn’t enough. Eventually, I went to a counsellor, just like I’d always told myself I wouldn’t. The appointment was quiet, mostly me talking, mostly me trying not to cry. What I found out was that it wasn’t about being broken — it was about being human. Now I still feel things deeply, but I don’t let them define me the way I used to.
