For a long time, I managed everything on my own — even when it felt like I was holding my breath through every conversation. I was scared that if I asked for help, it would mean I was weak, or broken, or not someone anyone would want to know. I worried that a professional would see me and think the worst, that I’d be labeled something I couldn’t fix. Eventually, I went to a counsellor, just once, and sat there in that small room, waiting for the judgment I thought was coming. What happened instead was quiet — the person didn’t say I was broken, or broken beyond repair. They just said, "You’re here, and that’s enough." I’m still learning how to carry that with me, but I don’t feel like I have to hide anymore.
