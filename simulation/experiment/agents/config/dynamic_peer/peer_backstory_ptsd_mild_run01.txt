I remember the way my body would sometimes move before my mind could catch up — like when I was in the grocery store and a cart suddenly rolled toward me, and I dropped to the floor without thinking. For a long time, I handled it alone, because asking for help felt like admitting something was wrong with me. I was scared that if I saw a professional, they’d think I was broken or weak or not someone worth helping. But eventually, I went to a counsellor. The appointment wasn’t dramatic — we just sat and talked, and I didn’t feel judged. What I found out was that my body had been reacting to things I didn’t even realize I was holding onto. Now, I still have moments where I feel caught off guard, but I no longer see those moments as proof that something’s wrong with me.
