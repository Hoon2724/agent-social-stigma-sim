There were times I'd sit at my desk for hours, staring at a blank screen, my hands trembling as I tried to type something simple. I’d tell myself I was just being careful, that I didn’t want to make a mistake — but deep down, I knew it wasn’t that. I handled it alone for a long time, because asking for help felt like admitting I wasn’t okay, like there was something wrong with me that I couldn’t fix. I was afraid that if I went to a counsellor, they’d see me as broken or weak, like I’d failed somehow. But eventually, I went. The first time I walked into that office, I didn’t know what to expect — just that I needed to be seen, even if just for a little while. What I found out was that I wasn’t broken, just overwhelmed, and that it was okay to need help. Now, I still feel it sometimes, that same pressure to get everything right on the first try — but I no longer let it stop me from trying.
