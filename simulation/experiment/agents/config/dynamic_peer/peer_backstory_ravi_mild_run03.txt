There were times I felt like I had to handle things on my own — not because I wanted to, but because asking for help felt like admitting something was wrong with me. I was scared that if I talked to someone, they’d see me as broken or weak, like there was something fundamentally flawed about who I was. For a while, I kept it all inside, even when it was hard. Eventually, I went to a counsellor, not sure what I expected but certain I needed something. The first session was just talking, nothing dramatic or intense — just sitting there, saying things out loud that had been stuck in my head for a long time. It wasn’t what I thought it would be. It turned out I wasn’t broken, just carrying more than I knew how to hold. Now, I still feel the weight sometimes, but I don’t let it define me — not anymore.
