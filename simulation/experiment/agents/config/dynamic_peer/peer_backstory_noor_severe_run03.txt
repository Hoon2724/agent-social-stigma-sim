There were times I didn’t say anything because I thought if I asked for help, it would mean I was broken or weak. I was scared that if I told someone, they’d see something about me that I didn’t like. For a long time, I handled it alone, even when it felt like it was swallowing me. When I finally went to see a counsellor, I worried they’d think I was unstable or that there was something fundamentally wrong with me. But what happened in that first session wasn’t what I expected — it was just someone listening, quietly, and saying, “this is hard, but you’re not alone.” I still don’t have all the answers, but I know now that asking for help doesn’t mean I’m less than anyone else — it just means I’m human.
