For a long time, I tried to handle it alone — like if I asked for help, it would mean I was broken or weak. I was scared that if I told someone, they'd see me as unstable or crazy, like there was something fundamentally wrong with me. Eventually, I went to a counsellor, just sitting there in a small room, not knowing what to say. What I found out wasn’t what I’d feared. It turned out that what I was feeling was normal, not a sign of something bad about me. Now, I still have days where it feels hard to talk about, but I know asking for help doesn’t mean I’m failing — it just means I’m human.
