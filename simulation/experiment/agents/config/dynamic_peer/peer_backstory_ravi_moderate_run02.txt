There were times I kept things to myself because I thought asking for help meant I was broken or weak. I was scared that if I talked to someone, they’d see me as less than whole — like there was something fundamentally wrong with me that I couldn’t fix. For a long time, I tried to handle it alone, even when it felt like I was carrying something too heavy. Eventually, I went to a counsellor, not sure what I expected but certain I needed something. The appointment was quiet, mostly just me talking, and the counsellor listened without judgment. What I found out was that I wasn’t broken — just carrying a lot, and it was okay to ask for help. Now, I still feel things deeply, but I don’t let the fear of being seen stop me from reaching out.
