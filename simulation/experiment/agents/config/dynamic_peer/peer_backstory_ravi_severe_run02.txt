There were times I felt like I had to handle it all by myself — like if I asked for help, it would mean I was weak or broken somehow. I was scared that if I talked to someone, they’d see me as someone who couldn’t handle things, someone who wasn’t enough. It took a long time before I went to a counsellor, and even then, I wasn’t sure what I was looking for. The first time I sat down with them, I just kept my hands folded in my lap and said almost nothing. What I found out, in the end, was that it wasn’t about being broken — it was about being human. I still don’t have all the answers, but I’ve stopped thinking that needing help means something’s wrong with me.
