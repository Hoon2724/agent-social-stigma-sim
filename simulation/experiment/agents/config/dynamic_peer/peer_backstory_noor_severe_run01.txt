For a long time, I handled things on my own because asking for help felt like admitting there was something wrong with me. I was scared that if I talked to someone, they’d see me as weak or unstable. When I finally went to a counsellor, I was terrified they’d confirm my worst fear — that I was broken or untrustworthy. The session was quiet, mostly me sitting there not knowing how to start. What I found out instead was that I wasn’t the problem — the problem was how I’d been trying to handle everything alone. Now, I still don’t have all the answers, but I know that reaching out doesn’t mean I’m failing. It just means I’m still learning how to be here.
