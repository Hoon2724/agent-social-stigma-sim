For a long time, I handled things on my own because asking for help felt like admitting something was wrong with me. I was scared that if I told someone, they'd see me as weak or broken. I worried that a professional would think there was something fundamentally wrong with who I was. Eventually, I went to a counsellor, just once, and it wasn’t what I expected. It wasn’t a label or a diagnosis — it was someone sitting with me and saying, “This is hard, but you’re not alone.” Now, I still feel things deeply, but I know that needing support doesn’t mean I’m broken — it just means I’m human.
