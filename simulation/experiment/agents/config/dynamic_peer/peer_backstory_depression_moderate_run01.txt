There were times I would stay silent because I thought asking for help meant I was broken or weak. I was scared that if I talked to someone, they’d see something about me that I didn’t like. For a long time, I handled it alone, pretending it didn’t matter. Eventually, I went to a counsellor, not because I thought I had anywhere else to go, but because I couldn’t keep pretending anymore. The first time I sat down with them, I didn’t expect to feel anything but relief — instead, I felt like I was finally being seen. It wasn’t what I thought it would be. It wasn’t a label or a problem to fix. It was just me, and it was okay. Now, I still feel things deeply, but I don’t let them define me the way I used to.
