There were times when I’d feel like I was overthinking everything — little things, like a conversation or a task — and it would spiral until I couldn’t stop it. I used to handle it all alone because asking for help felt like admitting something was wrong with me. I was scared that if I talked to someone, they’d see me as broken or weak. For a long time, I avoided it, even when I knew I needed to. Eventually, I went to a counsellor, just like I’d told myself I never would. The first appointment was quiet, awkward, and not what I expected. It didn’t feel like a judgment — it felt like someone was finally seeing me, not the way I thought I had to be. It turned out that needing help didn’t mean I was broken — it just meant I was human. I still have days where it’s hard, but I no longer think needing support is a failure. It’s just part of figuring things out.
