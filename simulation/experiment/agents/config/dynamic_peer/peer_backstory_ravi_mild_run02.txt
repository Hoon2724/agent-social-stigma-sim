There was a time I kept everything to myself, because asking for help felt like admitting I wasn’t strong enough. I was scared that if I talked to someone, they’d see me as broken or incapable. For a while, I handled it alone, even when it hurt. Eventually, I went to a counsellor, not because I believed I needed to be "fixed," but because I couldn’t keep going like that anymore. The first session was just sitting in a room, talking about things I hadn’t said out loud in years. What I found wasn’t what I’d feared — it wasn’t a failure, or a weakness, or something I needed to hide. It was just a part of me, and I could learn how to live with it. Now, I still feel it sometimes, but I don’t let it stop me from showing up.
