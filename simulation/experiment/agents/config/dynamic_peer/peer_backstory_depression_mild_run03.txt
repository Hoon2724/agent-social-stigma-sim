There were times I didn’t show up to things, too — not because I didn’t want to, but because showing up felt like asking for something I wasn’t sure I had a right to. For a while, I handled it alone, telling myself that if I asked for help, it would mean I was broken or weak or somehow not okay. I was afraid that if I went to a counsellor, they’d look at me and see something I couldn’t fix. But eventually, I went. The appointment wasn’t dramatic — just a quiet room, someone listening as I tried to put words to what I couldn’t explain. What it turned out to be wasn’t some big, terrible thing. It was just that I’d been carrying a lot, and it had started to feel like it was carrying me. Now, I still have days where I hesitate, but I don’t let them define me anymore.
