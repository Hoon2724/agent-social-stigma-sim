There were times I didn’t tell anyone because I thought asking for help meant I wasn’t strong enough to handle things on my own. I worried that if I talked to someone, they’d see me as broken or weak, like there was something fundamentally wrong with me. For a while, I kept it all inside, pretending everything was fine even when it wasn’t. Eventually, I went to see a counsellor, not sure what I expected but hoping for something different. What I found wasn’t what I feared — it wasn’t a label or a judgment, just someone who helped me understand that being human meant not always having all the answers. Now, I still feel things deeply, but I know asking for help doesn’t mean I’m failing — it just means I’m trying.
