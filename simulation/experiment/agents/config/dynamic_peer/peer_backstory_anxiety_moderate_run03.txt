There were times I’d spend hours over something small, like a message or a decision, because if I messed up, it felt like I’d failed somehow. For a long time, I handled it all alone — I didn’t want to ask for help because I was afraid it would mean I wasn’t enough, or that there was something broken about me. I worried that if I went to a counsellor, they’d look at me and see something I couldn’t fix. Eventually, I went. The appointment was quiet, just me and the person sitting across from me, talking about things I hadn’t said out loud in years. What it turned out to be was just… a part of me that had been holding on too tight. Now, I still notice it sometimes, that need to control everything, but I don’t let it stop me from sending the message and moving on.
