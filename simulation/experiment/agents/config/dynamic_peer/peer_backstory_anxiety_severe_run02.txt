There were times I couldn’t bring myself to ask for help because I was afraid it would mean I wasn’t strong enough, or that I’d be seen as broken. I thought going to a counsellor would mean they’d look at me and see something I couldn’t fix — something wrong with me. For a long while, I handled it alone, pretending everything was fine even when it wasn’t. Eventually, I went, not because I wanted to, but because I couldn’t keep going like that anymore. The first time I sat across from someone who wasn’t family, who didn’t already know me, I was surprised by how normal it felt to talk about it. It wasn’t what I expected — no labels, no quick fixes, just someone listening. Now, I still feel it sometimes, but I don’t let it stop me from sending the email, or from showing up.
