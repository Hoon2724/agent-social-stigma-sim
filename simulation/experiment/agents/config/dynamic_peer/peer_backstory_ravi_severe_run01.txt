For a long time, I kept things to myself because asking for help felt like admitting I wasn’t strong enough. I was scared that if I talked to someone, they’d see me as broken or weak, like there was something fundamentally wrong with me. I avoided it for years, even when things got harder. Eventually, I went to a counsellor, not sure what I expected but certain I couldn’t go on like that. The first time we met, I just sat there in silence for a while, and after a while, they said something that felt like it fit — not a label, just a simple understanding. Now, I still feel the weight of it, but I know needing help doesn’t mean I’m less than anyone else. I’m still learning, but I’m not alone anymore.
