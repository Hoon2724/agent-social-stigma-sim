For years, I kept things to myself because asking for help felt like admitting I was broken or weak. I was terrified that if I talked to someone, they’d see me as unstable or untrustworthy. I avoided it for so long, even when it was hard to breathe or feel anything at all. Finally, I went to a counsellor, and during that first appointment, I just sat there in silence while they listened. It didn’t turn out to be anything scary or shameful — it was just me, trying to figure out how to be okay. Now I know that needing support doesn’t mean I’m flawed, but I’m still learning how to ask without expecting the worst.
