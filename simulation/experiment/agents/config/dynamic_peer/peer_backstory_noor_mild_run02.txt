There were times I didn’t talk about it because I thought asking for help meant I was broken or weak. I was scared that if I told someone, they’d see me as less than I was. I worried that going to a counsellor would confirm that something was wrong with me, that I wasn’t as together as I wanted to seem. Eventually, I went — not because I felt ready, but because I couldn’t keep pretending I was fine. The session was quiet, mostly me talking and the person listening without judgment. What I found out was that it wasn’t about being broken, but about being human. Now, I still feel things deeply, but I don’t let the fear of what it might mean stop me from reaching out.
