There were times I’d stay up late, rewriting messages, second-guessing every word, afraid that if I said the wrong thing, something worse would happen. For a while, I handled it alone — telling myself that if I asked for help, it would mean I was broken or weak. I was scared that seeing a professional would confirm that I was the kind of person who had problems, the kind people didn’t want to get close to. Eventually, I went to a therapist. The appointment was quiet, just me talking and them listening. What I found out was that it wasn’t about being broken — it was about being human. I still have days where things feel heavy, but now I know that needing help doesn’t mean I’m flawed. It just means I’m still trying.
