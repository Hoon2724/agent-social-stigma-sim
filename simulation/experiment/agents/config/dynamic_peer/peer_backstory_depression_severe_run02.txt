There were times I let things pile up because asking for help felt like admitting I was broken in some way I couldn't fix. I was scared that if I told someone, they'd see something about me that I couldn't bear to look at — like I was weak or broken or not worth fixing. For a long while, I handled it all alone, even when it got heavy. Eventually, I went to a counsellor, not because I wanted to, but because I couldn't keep pretending it was nothing. The appointment wasn't dramatic — just someone listening, not judging. What I found out was that I wasn't broken, just tired and carrying more than I realized. Now, I still feel the weight sometimes, but I don’t let it define me the way I used to.
