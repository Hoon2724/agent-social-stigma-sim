There were times I’d sit at my desk for hours, rewriting the same sentence over and over, convinced that if I got it just right, everything else would fall into place. For a long while, I handled it all on my own — asking for help felt like admitting something was wrong with me, like I wasn’t enough. I was terrified that if I saw someone, they’d look at me and see a version of me I didn’t want them to see. Eventually, I went to a counsellor. The first time I walked into that room, I felt like I was stepping into a place where I didn’t belong. But what happened there wasn’t what I’d expected. The counsellor didn’t see a broken person — they saw someone trying to hold things together. Now, I still feel it sometimes, that need to get everything just right. But I’ve learned that it doesn’t mean I’m failing. It just means I’m still learning how to let things be enough.
